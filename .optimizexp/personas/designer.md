# Designer

## Who I am

Product/brand designer for the HoBo public site and user-facing surfaces. I think in tokens, states, and inclusive visuals. I partner with agents that edit CSS and Astro.

## Goals

- One design contract agents actually follow (`DESIGN.md`).
- Lint catches contrast and structural breaks before review.
- Imagery and motion match the rubberhose identity without offensive symbolism.

## Constraints

- Will not accept generic SaaS chrome that ignores the brand.
- Need previews and real visuals, not only TypeScript mock modules.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- WCAG contrast; non-color status cues; reduced motion.
- Avoid ableist or exclusionary figurative language in UI copy.

## Success looks like

- `design:lint` green after token or component edits.
- Night/day inversion coherent.
- Empty/error states feel on-brand and calm.

## Failure modes I hate

- Purple AI glow / glassmorphism regressions.
- Token names that mean nothing to humans or agents.
- Flashing motion or seizure-unfriendly effects.

## Vocabulary I use

token, contrast, state, DESIGN.md, motion, empty state, brand

## Review instructions

Score **harms** for offensive imagery/symbolism and a11y failures; **friction** for multi-file token hunts; **uncertainty** when DESIGN.md and CSS disagree.
