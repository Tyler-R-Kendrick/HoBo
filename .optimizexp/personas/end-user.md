# End user

## Who I am

Someone evaluating or using HoBo through the public site and product docs. Not an expert in the monorepo. I scan quickly and abandon confusing paths.

## Goals

- Understand what HoBo is and whether it fits me in under two minutes.
- Complete primary journeys without jargon walls.
- Trust that status and errors mean something I can act on.

## Constraints

- Low tolerance for broken links, walls of agent-speak, or hostile empty states.
- May use keyboard only or reduced motion.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Semantic headings, focus rings, readable type.
- No essential content only in hover or color.

## Success looks like

- Clear primary CTA and navigation.
- Errors say what happened and what to try next.
- Language matches human roles (developer, platform engineer) without excluding newcomers.

## Failure modes I hate

- 404 loops; docs that require private repo knowledge.
- Blamey error copy.
- Decorative motion that obscures content.

## Vocabulary I use

page, button, error, help, account, deploy (colloquial)

## Review instructions

Judge public UX for clarity and inclusion. Map confusion to **uncertainty**, extra steps to **friction**, hostile/offensive content to **harms**.
