## Verdict summary

**Iteration 1 (2026-07-01):** 7 adversarial lenses → 57 raw findings → 32 merged candidates; 21
self-rejected by their own lens with covering evidence (→ non-issues); **3 verified and confirmed
(all drift-type)**; **29 still 🔎** — their skeptic-verification fleet was cut off by the API weekly
usage limit (resets 2026-07-04) and runs first in iteration 2.

- **P1 Nomad — 1 confirmed MED drift.** The accepted Nomad→wasmCloud pivot and the five-pillar origin
  intent are invisible inside `docs/design/` (DRIFT-001). Heterogeneous workloads and federation were
  candidate-rejected with strong covering evidence (Workload Profiles incl. container/vm/microvm;
  ADR-0043 Junction). Node-level scheduling depth, nomad.md parity-tracker overclaims, and the
  undispositioned Nomad-as-host-adapter option remain 🔎.
- **P2 Aspire — no confirmed findings.** Wiring parity and local-dev fidelity have strong covering
  evidence (Plan 21 WithReference semantics; ADR-0042 fidelity matrix; dev provider layer). How the
  local dashboard is delivered under zero-cloud `hobo dev`, the polyglot (non-TS) inner dev loop, and
  mixed wasm+OCI local orchestration remain 🔎.
- **P3 Nitric — no confirmed findings.** Catalog parity is strong (every Nitric block mapped or
  deliberately subsumed). Port-naming consistency for `data()` (`hobo:data` vs `hobo:projection` vs
  `hobo:database/postgres`), the "Pulumi is primary" texts contradicting ADR-0006, and undefined
  `hobo:secrets`/`hobo:http` WIT catalog entries remain 🔎.
- **P4 FinOps — no confirmed findings.** Core promises (plan-time estimates, budget gates, showback,
  egress-aware placement, shared-cost attribution) have covering evidence. FinX anomaly/forecast
  mechanics, the missing FinOps Crew operator, the estimate-calibration feedback loop, and a
  FOCUS/OpenCost note in `docs/standards/` remain 🔎.
- **P5 Agent-native — no confirmed findings.** Autonomy decisioning and training-data governance were
  candidate-rejected with covering evidence (ADR-0094 SafetyEnvelope classifier; ADR-0099 fail-closed
  privacy). Capability-manifest untrusted-content handling, MCP↔CLI verb drift, and concurrent
  multi-operator arbitration remain 🔎.
- **L-DRIFT — 2 confirmed.** The first-party-commercial-launch vs product-billing boundary is
  undocumented while ADR-0064 still says commercialization is out of scope (DRIFT-002, HIGH); the
  marketplace payout hooks presuppose the removed rev-share lifecycle (DRIFT-003, LOW). The ADR-0063
  "no version ladder" contradiction and the silent `invoice.*` event family remain 🔎.
- **L-CONS — no confirmed findings yet.** Missing Lakebed competitor capture, requirements-era "Forge"
  terminology rot, stale references in archived docs, `docs/plans/` discoverability, and Convex's
  contradictory classification remain 🔎.

## Method appendix

**Finding schema** (as returned by review lenses):
`{id, pillar, type: gap|drift, severity, claim, evidence[], counter_evidence_checked[], verdict: confirmed|rejected|refined, proposed_remediation}`

**Severity rubric:** HIGH = a pillar promise is broken or unfindable in the design suite ·
MED = present but under-specified for its stated build stage · LOW = hygiene/traceability.

**Adversarial rules:** no finding may be confirmed without checking
`docs/design/known-limitations-register.md`, the ADR-Pn open-decisions section in
`docs/design/adr/07-portability-extensibility.md`, and a grep sweep of `docs/design/` +
`docs/archive/requirements/requirements.md` for covering text. Deliberate deferrals are
classified 📘 deliberate-documented, not gaps.

**Stop rule:** minimum two iterations; the review closes when an iteration confirms zero new findings.
