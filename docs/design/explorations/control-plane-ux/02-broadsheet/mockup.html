GitOps Crew Promotes harbor-crm v2.5.0 to Production Across Three Camps; Canary Clean in 14 Minutes

Plan #4182 cleared four policy gates without a human page; Sarah’s standing approval covered the blast radius. The event log recorded 312 entries, every one replayable.

By Gitops Crew, with SRE Crew · Junction acme-industries

The promotion began at 02:14 UTC, when the GitOps Crew observed commit 9f3e2a1 land on harbor-crm’s release branch and opened Plan #4182 against the production Bindle. The plan proposed 27 changes across three Camps — wasmcloud-fra1 (self-hosted), hetzner-hel1, and fly-iad — touching the api, web, and worker services while leaving postgres, the events queue, and the blob store untouched. The plan diff ran to 118 lines; nothing in it was novel.

Policy gates cleared in sequence. Capability-diff found no new capabilities requested. Secretless-audit confirmed that every reference resolves to a vault handle — zero inline secrets, as has been the standard since March. Cost-ceiling projected +$1.12 per day, well under the $25 threshold. Conformance ran 147 of 147 tests green against each Camp’s contract. Sarah Okafor’s standing approval for minor-version promotions covered the blast radius, and no human was paged.

The canary took the longer road. SRE Crew held 5 percent of traffic on v2.5.0 at fly-iad for 14 minutes, watching p95 latency — it held at 88 ms against a 120 ms budget — and the error rate, which stayed at 0.02 percent. One warning surfaced: a transient queue-consumer lag of 340 ms at 02:21, which the crew ruled ordinary rebalancing and filed as event #4182.207 rather than a rollback trigger.

Full rollout completed at 02:47 UTC. The log holds 312 entries for the promotion; an operator who wishes to see the deployment again may replay the window 02:14–02:47 against any Camp. Plan diff, gate transcripts, and canary traces are filed under Plan #4182.

Notable in the margins: this was the first production promotion carrying a commit by Marcus Webb, who joined Tuesday. Mr. Webb touched no Camp directly — the Bindle abstraction held — and his commit message (“fix invoice rounding, again”) now sits in the permanent record.

Asked for comment, the SRE Crew’s closing entry reads, in full: “Nominal.”

Capability Review

Morgan Signs Off on Narrowed Blob-Store Grant

Security Desk

Security review of harbor-crm’s capability manifest concluded Thursday. Morgan Reyes approved the api service’s request to write to the blob store, but narrowed it: the grant covers the invoices/ prefix only, expires in 90 days, and is secretless — the service holds a reference, never a key.

Two requests were declined: worker’s ask for open egress to *.stripe.com, routed instead through the payments proxy, and a wildcard read left over from v1. “Bindles ask for capabilities,” the review note reads. “They do not get credentials.”
