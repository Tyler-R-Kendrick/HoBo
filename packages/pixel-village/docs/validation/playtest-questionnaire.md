# Playtest Questionnaire (post-session, ~5 min)

12 Likert items, 1 = strongly disagree … 7 = strongly agree. Adapted
short-form GEQ (competence, flow, tension, challenge, affect) + PENS-style
autonomy/relatedness + console-trust items. Record answers in the session
score file under `questionnaire:`.

## Game experience (GEQ short form, adapted)

1. I felt skillful operating the yard. *(competence)*
2. I felt capable of handling whatever the yard raised. *(competence)*
3. I forgot the time while working the board. *(flow)*
4. I was fully occupied with the yard, not with the UI. *(flow)*
5. I felt tense or frustrated during the session. *(tension — reverse-scored)*
6. The yard pressured me to act faster than I wanted. *(tension — reverse-scored; TRU-3 signal)*
7. Clearing the board felt worth doing. *(positive affect)*
8. Reaching the Highball felt earned, not handed out. *(challenge/affect)*

## Needs satisfaction (PENS-style)

9. I chose what to do next; the yard never forced an order on me. *(autonomy)*
10. Bo and the yard's characters made the system feel approachable without
    getting in my way. *(relatedness)*

## Console trust

11. I trusted that what the yard showed me matched reality.
12. I could always tell simulated data from real data.

## Open questions (verbatim answers)

- What was the most confusing moment? *(maps to glanceability/onboarding)*
- Where did the game get in the way of the job? *(maps to core-loop LOOP-3)*
- What would make you open this instead of the CLI tomorrow? *(adoption)*
