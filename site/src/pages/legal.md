no cookies, embeds no tracking pixels, and contains no forms. It does not collect,

## Using HoBo with third-party providers

HoBo routes work to the cloud and CI providers **you** register, using credentials
**you** supply for accounts **you** hold. Your own agreement with each provider
governs that usage, including its free-tier and acceptable-use terms.

HoBo never holds a provider account on your behalf, never pools or resells free-tier
capacity across users, and never creates or rotates accounts to reset a free tier.
That boundary is a recorded architectural decision, not just a practice — see
[](https://github.com/Tyler-R-Kendrick/HoBo/blob/main/).
