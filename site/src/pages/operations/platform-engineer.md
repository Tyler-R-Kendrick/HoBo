# Platform Engineer (Crew)

Authors and proposes **platform** changes: resource definitions, packs, policy pack deltas, Matching hints — not day-to-day business features (that is SWE).

## Mission

| Does | Does not |
|---|---|
| Propose policy packs | Silently weaken mandatory deny ceilings |
| Draft resource definitions | Bypass Registry conformance |
| Suggest Matching/Driver changes | Apply prod without obligations when required |
