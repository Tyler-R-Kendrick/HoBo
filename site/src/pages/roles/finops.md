# HoBo for FinOps

You care about **unit economics and accountability** without becoming the team that maintains every cloud console.

## What HoBo gives you

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Metering facts + showback dimensions | Tenant/project/run attribution |
| Continuous migration | Rent trends down when policy allows |
| Policy budgets / breakers | Enforcement, not vibes |
| FinOps Crew | Always-on anomaly and proposal assistant |
| Dated ranking sources | Honest price inputs |

## What you should demand

1. No free-tier pooling stories.  
2. No fabricated “vendor signed” price attestations.  
3. Replayable dispute lineage from invoice-adjacent views to facts.  
4. Clear separation of **ranking** vs **billing**.
